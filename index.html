Frequently Asked Questions

How to crop and compress photo under 50KB for SSC & UPSC?

Tap the "SSC / UPSC Photo" preset, align your face in the crop window, and click Generate. The binary compression engine will guarantee the size stays under 50KB.

What is the Magic Signature Ink Boost?

It automatically enhances contrast and removes shadows from signatures photographed in poor light, giving a crisp black-and-white scan required by exam portals.

Are my personal photos and signatures safe?

Yes, 100%. All processing occurs strictly inside your web browser. No image data is ever transferred across the internet.
